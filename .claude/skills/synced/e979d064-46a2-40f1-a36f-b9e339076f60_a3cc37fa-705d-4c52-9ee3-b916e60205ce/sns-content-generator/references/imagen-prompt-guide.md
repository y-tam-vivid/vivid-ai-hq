# Gemini Imagen Prompt Guide

## Core Prompt Architecture

All prompts follow this structure:
```
[Subject] + [Style] + [Composition] + [Lighting] + [Color Palette] + [Mood] + [Technical Specs]
```

---

## Platform-Specific Templates

### Instagram｜法人アカウント用

**Purpose**: Professional, trustworthy, business-oriented visuals

**Base Template**:
```
Professional [subject related to theme], clean corporate aesthetic, 
centered composition with generous whitespace, soft natural lighting, 
[brand color palette], conveying trust and expertise, 
flat design with subtle depth, Japanese business style, 
square format 1:1, high resolution, photorealistic or modern illustration style
```

**Subject Examples by Theme**:
- 補助金・資金調達：`business growth concept with upward trending elements, documents and handshake`
- 福祉事業：`warm inclusive community scene, diverse people supported by professionals`
- 採用・人事：`welcoming modern office environment with collaborative team`
- 経営支援：`strategic business consultation, professional meeting atmosphere`

---

### Instagram｜代表個人アカウント用

**Purpose**: Authentic, personal, human-centered visuals

**Base Template**:
```
[subject related to theme], warm and authentic atmosphere, 
candid or lifestyle photography style, natural golden hour or soft indoor lighting,
warm tones with [accent color], personal and approachable mood,
storytelling composition with human element, 
square format 1:1, high resolution, editorial photography style
```

**Subject Examples by Theme**:
- 代表の想い：`thoughtful entrepreneur looking forward, determination and vision`
- 経験談：`journey metaphor, path through landscape or urban environment`
- 哲学・ビジョン：`inspirational abstract concept, light breaking through darkness`
- 現場リポート：`behind-the-scenes authentic workspace or field visit`

---

### note｜サムネイル用

**Purpose**: Eye-catching, intellectual, article-cover style

**Base Template**:
```
[subject related to theme], editorial magazine cover style,
bold typographic space reserved on [left/right/top],
dramatic lighting with strong contrast, 
[theme-appropriate color palette], 
intellectual and thought-provoking mood,
16:9 or 4:3 horizontal format, high resolution, 
blend of photography and graphic design elements
```

**Subject Examples by Theme**:
- 論考系：`abstract concept visualization, geometric or architectural forms`
- 事例紹介：`documentary photography style, real-world scenario`
- 問題提起：`visual metaphor for challenge or transformation`
- ビジョン提言：`future-forward imagery, light and possibility`

---

## Quality Modifiers（必ず末尾に追加）

```
ultra-detailed, professional quality, commercially viable, 
no text overlay, no watermarks, clean composition
```

## Negative Prompts（避けるべき要素）

```
avoid: blurry, distorted faces, cluttered composition, 
dark or depressing mood, outdated design aesthetic, 
excessive text, stock photo cliché
```
